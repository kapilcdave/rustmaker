# PREREG — Sardine causal BTC probability transfer

Frozen before joining or scoring the files.

Sardine publishes one causal BTC-15m probability at 720, 480, 300 and 120
seconds remaining, based on spot distance and a pre-window volatility scale.
The October 6 public history contains 7,544 windows beginning July 19. This
test does not fit or recalibrate that model.

Join Sardine rows by exact Kalshi ticker and checkpoint to the independent
local September 14–October 5 actual bid/ask panel. In chronological checkpoint
order `(720, 480, 300, 120)`, calculate the model's net edge for buying YES
and NO at the local actual side ask and exact one-contract taker fee. Buy the
better side at the first checkpoint with at least 3c modeled edge, once per
window, and hold to settlement.

Pass requires at least 100 trades, positive day-clustered 95% lower bound,
positive chronological halves, positive after an extra 1c execution stress,
positive after deleting the best 10% of days, and no day above 20% of gross
positive P&L. The source model and local execution panel remain paper
evidence; no order is authorized.
